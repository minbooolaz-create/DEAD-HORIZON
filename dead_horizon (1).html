THE COLLAPSE

The dead woke up without warning. In a single night, the towns of New Sonrn County fell silent — and what didn't stay silent started walking.

JOKYLAND, NEW SONRN

You and your friends wake up in a dilapidated farmhouse on the edge of Jokyland, a rural stretch of New Sonrn County. The power is out. The roads are empty. A dead pickup truck sits rusting in the yard.

The radio still crackles — a repeating emergency broadcast from the overseas nation of Harban, promising one final extraction point out of the quarantine zone.

THE LONG ROAD

To reach the coast you'll need to secure a safehouse, scavenge guns and supplies, rebuild a broken-down muscle car piece by piece — engine, battery, tires — then fuel it up and drive the highway south, through the fallen city of Markaz, before the final crossing to Harban.

HARBAN & SABOBOLAX

Across the sea, Harban still holds a corridor open at its capital, Sabobolax — a misty, sprawling metropolis where the last extraction helicopter waits. Reach the rooftop. Signal the pilot. Get out alive.
