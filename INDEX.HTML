THE COLLAPSE

The dead woke up without warning. In a single night, the towns of New Sonrn County fell silent — and what didn't stay silent started walking.

JOKYLAND, NEW SONRN

You wake up in a dilapidated farmhouse on the edge of Jokyland. The power is out. The roads are empty. A dead pickup truck sits rusting in the yard.

The radio still crackles — a broadcast from the overseas nation of Harban, promising one final extraction point out of the quarantine zone.

THE LONG ROAD

Rebuild the broken-down car piece by piece — engine, battery, tires — fuel it up, and drive south through the fallen city of Markaz before the crossing to Harban.

HARBAN, SABOBOLAX & AXEWOOD

Across the sea, Harban holds a corridor open at its capital, Sabobolax. But the extraction zone isn't safe either — you'll need to steal a car and make one last run to the district of Axewood to complete your escape.
